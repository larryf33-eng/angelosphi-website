About Angelos C. Painting Inc.

A painting and remodeling company built in Ossining, NY on 25 years of careful work and repeat customers.

Our story

Built on craftsmanship and trust

Angelos C. Painting Inc. is a professional painting and remodeling company based in Ossining, NY, dedicated to delivering reliable and high-quality services to residential and commercial clients.

Over more than 25 years, we've built the business one job at a time — on referrals and repeat customers. We combine hands-on expertise with a customer-centered approach to deliver efficient, detail-oriented solutions that enhance property value and increase client satisfaction.

We're available 24/7 with flexible scheduling and complimentary estimates, serving Rockland, Westchester, Orange and Putnam Counties.
